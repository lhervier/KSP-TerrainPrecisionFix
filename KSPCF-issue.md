# The terrain collision surface is rebuilt at a different height on every load

**Every time a save is loaded, KSP builds the collision mesh under your craft at a different height.**
Load one save six times: KSP puts the capsule back to the micrometre, yet where it settles spreads
13.5 cm on Kerbin. No load is even needed: drive away from a parked craft and come back, and the
ground under it has moved.

**How this was made, and why this is an issue and not a PR.** The diagnosis and a patch were written
with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. Changes generated by an AI, proposed by people who cannot weigh
what they imply, are a real burden on open-source projects right now, and I do not want to add to
yours. So I did not believe any step of the diagnosis until a measurement agreed with it, the patch
lives in a mod of its own, and I am not proposing it for KSPCF. I write Java backends for a living, I
am not a Unity developer, and this is the first time I have gone this far into KSP's internals. What
I am asking for is your opinion: whether the diagnosis holds, and what a patch like this could break
that I have not thought of.

## Repro

![A craft jumping on its own the moment a save is reloaded](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFix/raw/master/imgs/Booing-scaled.gif)

*Stock 1.12.5 with KSPCF as the only mod, `PartStartStability` included. A pod on an empty fuel tank,
parked in the grass at the KSC, saved, then reloaded from the pause menu, several times if needed.*

To measure it, add [Terrain Precision Fix Diag 1](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFixDiag):
an instrument of about 200 lines, no Harmony, no patching. It reads the distance from the craft to the
centre of the body as the save hands it back, and once it has settled. Six loads of one save: the
first reading repeats to within three micrometres, the second spreads **134.5 mm on Kerbin**, 20.7 mm
on the Mun, 6.7 mm on Minmus, 3.3 mm on Gilly
([readings](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFix/blob/master/docs/checking-the-culprit-loading.md)).

[Diag 2](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFixDiag2), just as inert, reads the ground with
no craft involved: a ray straight down against the height `pqsController` computes there. Over six
loads on Kerbin the collision surface moves 108.1 mm, the computed height 0.039 mm.

The same happens in flight. Drive a rover past 2.5 km from a parked pod and back, six times in one
flight: the surface under the pod comes back over 21.8 mm
([readings](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFix/blob/master/docs/checking-the-culprit-approach.md)).

## What's going on

Max level quads hang from `LocalSpacePQStorage`, and their position is a 600 km double stored in a
float:

```csharp
// PQ.PreciseUpdateSubQuadsPosition; the same two values are computed in PQ.Subdivide
positionPlanetRelative = positionPlanePosition.normalized;              // Vector3d
positionPlanet = positionPlanetRelative * sphereRoot.GetSurfaceHeight(positionPlanetRelative);
quadTransform.localPosition = positionPlanet;
```

Each vertex goes through the same kind of round trip in `PQS.BuildVertexSurfaceRelative`. One float
step is 62.5 mm at 600 km, and every spread above is one or two steps.

A rounding alone would repeat. This one does not, because what the doubles are rounded into — the
world matrix of the terrain sphere — is not the same twice: its rotation (`directRotAngle`) comes back
off by however long you played since the save, and its translation moves at every floating origin
shift. [Diag 3](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFixDiag3) shows both. The whole
reasoning, with the code quoted:
[The culprit](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFix/blob/master/docs/the-culprit.md).

## The mod

[Terrain Precision Fix](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFix) places the terrain in
double, and is explained in the first comment below. Same install, same readings: 134.5 mm → 0.004 mm
for the craft on Kerbin, 108.1 mm → 0.004 mm for the ground, 21.8 mm → 0.011 mm in flight.

It moves the ground everyone stands on, so every mod that relies on it has to be checked.
[Limits and solutions](https://github.com/lhervier/KSP-TerrainPrecisionFix/blob/master/docs/limits-and-solutions.md)
lists what is checked, affected, or still to do — Kopernicus, Principia, Parallax, Deferred, existing
saves… It is surely incomplete, and anything you see missing from it is exactly the help I am after.
